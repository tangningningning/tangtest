As illustrated in Fig. 1, we consider a cross-domain marine
network composed of a single offshore base station, denoted
as b, S USVs, represented by the set S = {1, 2,...,S }, and A

a two-stage acoustic-RF cooperative system, where USVs act
as gateways between the underwater acoustic network and the
RF network, facilitating communication between the offshore
base station and the AUVs operating underwater. In this sys-
tem, two types of transmissions are involved: task-related and
service-related. Task-related transmissions follow a bottom-up
offloading process, where AUVs offload their tasks to nearby
USVs via underwater acoustic communication (UAC), and if
necessary, the tasks are further forwarded to the base station
via RF links. In contrast, service-related transmissions occur
during the content caching phase and follow a top-down process.
Specifically, services are pushed from the base station to USVs
through RF links and then forwarded to AUVs via UAC. To
visually differentiate these two processes, Fig.1 uses pink arrows
to represent service transmissions.

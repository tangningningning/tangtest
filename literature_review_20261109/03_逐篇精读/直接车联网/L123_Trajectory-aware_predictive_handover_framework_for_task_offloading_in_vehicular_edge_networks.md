Problem formulation
The main aim of our proposed MAPHO framework is to optimize 
task offloading decisions in a VEC environment by minimizing total 
offloading cost. The total offloading cost comprises spectrum usage 
costs, transmission expenses, and computation resource usage costs at 
the offloading destinations. In addition, the handover overhead cost is 
added when the vehicle executing the task is out of communication 
coverage.

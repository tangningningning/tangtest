# Cache Trigger Operation Readout Geometry Diagnosis

## Scope and method

This diagnosis distinguishes three mechanisms for the weak Cache trigger state discrimination: common-mode threshold miscalibration, operation-weight direction misalignment, and lack of a cross-realization linearly usable ΔQ signal in the encoder. It directly reuses the 576 saved onset-state representations and symmetric expected-Q values. No environment or exact-Q branch was executed.

The 16 mixed/error heads use leave-one-realization-out four-fold CV. Every outer fold trains on 24 samples from three realizations and tests on all eight slots of the held-out realization. Ridge strength is selected inside the outer training set with realization-held-out inner CV over the fixed grid `0.1, 1, 10, 100`. Every centering mean is computed from the relevant training fold only.

## Required conclusions

`LINEAR_Q_SIGNAL_PRESENT_IN_ENCODER = NO`

`COMMON_MODE_THRESHOLD_MISCALIBRATION = NO`

`OPERATION_WEIGHT_DIRECTION_MISALIGNED = NO`

`ENCODER_LINEAR_Q_SIGNAL_INSUFFICIENT = YES`

## Direct mechanism

**C. Encoder lacks a cross-realization linearly usable ΔQ signal.**

The common-mode offset is large relative to state variation (median ratio `40.836`), so it explains probability saturation. It does not explain the failed ΔQ ranking: median production AUC is `0.487`, and threshold-only correction has median per-head accuracy gain `-0.078`. Threshold changes cannot repair the ranking.

Actual-vs-probe weight cosine is low (median `0.044`), but the probe itself does not generalize: median out-of-fold AUC `0.415`, R² `-0.114`, and only `2/16` heads reach AUC 0.65. Therefore low cosine is not evidence for a better, stable linear direction that production failed to learn.

## Decision criteria

- **LINEAR_Q_SIGNAL_PRESENT_IN_ENCODER**: YES requires median out-of-fold probe AUC >=0.65, median out-of-fold R2 >0, and at least 10/16 heads with probe AUC >=0.65.

- **COMMON_MODE_THRESHOLD_MISCALIBRATION**: YES requires median production AUC >=0.65, median current sign accuracy <=0.60, median threshold-only accuracy gain >=0.10, and gain >=0.10 in at least 10/16 heads.

- **OPERATION_WEIGHT_DIRECTION_MISALIGNED**: YES requires linear signal present, median probe-minus-production AUC >=0.10, AUC gain >=0.10 in at least 10/16 heads, and median |actual-vs-probe cosine| <=0.30.

- **ENCODER_LINEAR_Q_SIGNAL_INSUFFICIENT**: YES requires median out-of-fold probe AUC <=0.60, median out-of-fold R2 <=0, and fewer than 8/16 heads with probe AUC >=0.65.
